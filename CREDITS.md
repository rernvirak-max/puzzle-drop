# Photo credits

Puzzle Drop uses real photographs. The files in `public/photos/` come from
Wikimedia Commons and are resized so the long edge is about 1080 pixels.
The fish amok photograph is 800 pixels wide because that is the original;
it was not enlarged. The game crops each photo in the browser when it cuts
the picture into tiles.

A photo chosen with **Use my photo** stays on the device and is not listed here.
